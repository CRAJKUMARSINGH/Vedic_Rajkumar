Weekly Prompt Templates for Kiro, Devin, Trae, and Antigravity
These prompts are based on the prescribed weekly plan for enriching the Vedic_Rajkumar app. They keep the same priority order: accuracy first, then security and production hardening, then core flow polish, then a small number of high-value features.
How to use: Paste each prompt into the corresponding tool at the start of the week. Replace any placeholders with your actual repo path, preferred branch name, and any known constraints. Review outputs weekly so you do not expand scope too early.
