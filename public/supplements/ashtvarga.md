# Ashtakavarga: How It Reads Your Entire Life

**Source**: Thread by @TvamAstrology on X (based on Brihat Jataka by Varahamihira, Chapter IX)

## What is Ashtakavarga?

- "Ashta" = 8
- "Varga" = division/score

Every planet gets scored from 8 different positions:
- The 7 planets
- The Ascendant (Lagna)

Each position either blesses a house or does not. The score per house = how much power that planet carries there.

## Why Do Advanced Astrologers Rely on It?

Your Lagna chart says **WHERE** a planet sits.  
Ashtakavarga tells you **HOW STRONG** it actually is there.

Two people can have Jupiter in the 5th house.  
One has 6 points. One has 2 points.  
Completely different results in life.

## Two Kinds of Ashtakavarga

1. **Bhinnashtakavarga (BAV)**  
   - Individual chart for each planet  
   - 7 planets = 7 separate charts  
   - Max score per house = 8 points

2. **Sarvashtakavarga (SAV)**  
   - All 7 BAV charts added together  
   - One master chart of all 12 houses  
   - Total always adds up to 337 points

The SAV is your life map.

## Step 1: Find the Benefic Houses

Take one planet (e.g., **Sun**).  
From the Sun's position, these houses are BENEFIC:  
→ 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th, 11th

**From Mars and Saturn**: Same houses as above.  
**From Venus**: 6th, 7th, 12th  
**From Jupiter**: 5th, 6th, 9th, 11th  
And so on for Moon, Mercury, Lagna.

Each benefic position = 1 dot (point).

## Step 2: Count the Dots

Go through all 12 signs.  
For each sign, count how many of the 8 positions give it a benefic dot.

That count (0 to 8) is the Sun's Ashtakavarga score for that sign.

Do this for all 7 planets.  
You now have 7 individual BAV tables.

Add them all up sign by sign → That is your **SAV chart**.

## The Two Purifications (Reductions)

Varahamihira says raw scores need two corrections.

**Trikona Reduction**:  
- Compare Aries-Leo-Sagittarius (and other trine groups)  
- Subtract the lowest score from all three  
- Evens out inherited strengths

**Ekadhipatya Reduction**:  
- Compare both signs of the same planet  
- Adjusts for single-owner signs

After these, the BAV table is ready for prediction.

## The Golden Rule of SAV

Varahamihira states clearly:  
- **30+ points** in a sign = strongly benefic results  
- **25 to 30** = neutral. Neither good nor bad.  
- **Below 25** = difficult. Challenges expected.

This rule applies to **ANY** planet transiting that sign.  
Not just natal. Transit + SAV score = prediction.

## What Each Planet's BAV Reveals

**Sun's BAV**  
- Zero-score signs = avoid starting new work during Sun's transit there  
- Disease, misery, and conflict expected

**Moon's BAV**  
- Zero-score signs = avoid starting work during Moon's transit  
- Location of the bathroom in your home determined by highest-score direction

**Mars's BAV**  
- Zero-score signs = Saturn transiting there causes disease or danger  
- Reveals position of the kitchen in your home

**Mercury's BAV**  
- Zero-score signs = Saturn there = loss of son or close friends

**Jupiter's BAV**  
- The score in the 5th house from Jupiter = number of children expected  
- Planets in enemy/depression signs = early death of children

**Venus's BAV**  
- When Venus transits signs with the highest score = marriage, wealth, land

**Saturn's BAV**  
- Scores from Ascendant to Saturn + from Saturn back = years of disease

## SAV Analysis: Life's Story in Numbers

Analyzing three sections of life:  
- **Signs Pisces to Gemini** = first third of life  
- **Signs Cancer to Libra** = middle years  
- **Signs Scorpio to Aquarius** = final years  

The section with the **highest SAV total** = happiest phase of life.  

Three or more malefic planets in one section? That third of your life carries real suffering.

## The Wealth Formula in SAV

Varahamihira gives this exact rule:  
- **11th house score > 10th** = gain exceeds effort  
- **12th house score < 11th** = losses are smaller than gains  
- **Ascendant score > 12th** = protection of wealth  

All three conditions met? → The person lives happily and prospers.  
Any condition reversed? → Hard work without reward.

## How to Time Events with Ashtakavarga

When a planet transits a sign:  
- High BAV score for that planet = excellent results  
- Upachaya (3rd/6th/10th/11th) **AND** high score = full benefic result  
- Low score **AND** Apachaya = full malefic result  

This is why Saturn's Sade Sati hits some people hard and others barely feel it. It depends on the BAV scores of the signs Saturn is passing through.

## Ashtakavarga (in 3 Lines)
- Every house has a score (0 to 8 per planet)  
- Higher the score = more support from that planet  
- Low score = planet struggles there regardless of sign or dignity

This is the one tool most hobbyist astrologers skip. Advanced practitioners never skip it.
