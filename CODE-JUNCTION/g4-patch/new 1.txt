### Takeaways for Vedic_Rajkumar

1. **Lahiri is the universal default** — matching the repo's own DoD ("sidereal Lahiri ayanamsa unless explicitly Western") and `ayanamsaEngine.ts`. Already aligned.
2. **Multi-ayanamsa selection is table stakes at the pro end** — the repo already has a 5-system engine; exposing it in the UI is cheap differentiation.
3. **Swiss Ephemeris is the accuracy gold standard**, and the repo has it built (`swissEphemerisService.ts`) but **off by default** because of the AGPL-3.0 licence — that is exactly decision **P3**; competitors either accept AGPL (JHora) or buy the commercial licence (Parashara's Light).
4. **Market accuracy tolerance is ±0.02°** — the repo's CI gate (P5) tightens to 0.01°, which would exceed the norm its competitors publish; the J2000 anchor difference (23.85472° in-engine vs ICRC 23.853222°, ≈5.4″) is within tolerance and should be documented in the gate.
