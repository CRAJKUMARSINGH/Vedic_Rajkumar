**3. Sabse mehnga decision jo aap aaj le sakte ho: sab kuch pehle din se "proper" karna.** Pvt Ltd, trademark, SOC 2 platform, pentest — sab 0 users par kharidna. Aap ₹5L kharch karoge aur pehla customer phir bhi nahi aayega. Order yeh hai: **infra minimum, launch, phir ek customer, phir legal.**

**4. Ek cost jo kisi table mein nahi aati: aapki salary.** Agar aap full-time ho, toh yeh ₹8–20L/saal hai aur woh infra se 20x hai. Isliye ek bhi decision jo aapko "₹300/mahina bachayega par 20 ghante lega" wala hai — woh galat hai. 20 ghante distribution par lagao, woh ₹300 se zyada laayega.
